HQ Studio: Terms of Service

Last updated: 10 November 2026

What HQ Studio is

HQ Studio is a private publishing tool run by one person (the owner) on their own computer. After the owner approves a finished video, it sends that video to the owner's own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok). It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and nobody else can use it.

Who may use it

Only the owner, and only with accounts the owner controls. Connecting an account means the owner signs in to that platform and grants HQ Studio permission to upload videos to it.

Platform rules

Every upload follows the terms and community guidelines of the platform it goes to, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Developer Terms. HQ Studio never posts anything without the owner's explicit approval. On TikTok, videos are only sent to the owner's inbox as drafts; the owner publishes them in the TikTok app.

Content

The owner is responsible for the videos and captions they publish and holds the rights to them.

No warranty

HQ Studio is provided as is, without any warranty. It may change or stop at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings, Security, Manage app permissions). HQ Studio then stops posting to that account.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
